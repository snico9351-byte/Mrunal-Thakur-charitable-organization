About Mrunal Thakur

A Heart That Acts Beyond the Screen

Mrunal Thakur was born on 1 August 1992 in Dhule, Maharashtra, India. From a small-town girl with big dreams, she rose to become one of India’s most beloved actresses, known for powerful performances in films such as Love Sonia, Super 30, Sita Ramam and Hi Nanna.

Her journey into cinema began with Love Sonia (2018), a film that exposed the dark realities of human trafficking. That experience left a permanent mark on her soul. She has spoken about how the film “sparked change, inspired NGOs and saved lives.” She later participated in a United Nations panel on the human cost of conflict-related sexual violence and trafficking — using her voice for those who have none.

Beyond the camera, Mrunal has quietly supported causes close to her heart: menstrual hygiene awareness so that girls stay in school, food drives for stray animals, and during the COVID-19 pandemic she mobilised fans to donate PPE kits to rural hospitals in Maharashtra. She believes that true success is measured by the lives we touch.

Yet one person cannot heal the world alone. Through Mrunal Charity Organisation, she invites every compassionate soul to join hands and bring light to orphans, widows and war survivors who need us most — especially in India, and also in Palestine, Haiti and other lands where suffering is heavy.

Our Mission

Mrunal Charity Organisation exists to restore dignity, provide safety, food, education and medical care to the most vulnerable: orphans, widows and victims of war and conflict.

🧒

Orphans

Children who have lost their parents to poverty, disease or violence. We provide shelter, nutrition, schooling and the love every child deserves — with a special focus on India.

👩

Widows

Women left alone after conflict or hardship. We offer livelihood support, counselling and a community that refuses to let them be forgotten.

🕊️

War Survivors

Families torn apart by conflict in places like Palestine, Haiti and beyond. Emergency relief, medical aid and long-term rebuilding of hope.

🇮🇳

India First

While our heart is global, the majority of our efforts remain rooted in India — the land that raised Mrunal and the millions who still wait for a chance.
